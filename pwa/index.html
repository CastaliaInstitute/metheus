OPTIONAL · SHARED ROOM AIMētheus AI＋

The bot processes only explicit !metheus ask or !metheus ask-chart messages, and only after every currently joined person has read its disclosure and opted in. Ordinary room chat is not sent to the model. A one-time chart-aware request sends the gates you provide for that turn. You can also explicitly enable your gates for your own later !metheus ask turns with “Use my gates for future asks”; check or clear that setting with the controls below. On every chart-aware turn, Mētheus receives your full selected gate list, derived channel map, and activities linked to those gates. It matches those activities to your current question, rather than assuming every gate is relevant. It answers your question first, can shape practical help around a relevant activity from your selected gates, and may offer one partner-gate activity if it clearly adds a useful option. It can skip chart framing when no activity fits. This is a self-reported symbolic chart context, not a verified chart or live awareness of transits, feelings, or body state; the bot does not remember earlier chart-aware replies. The setting stays in bot memory until you clear it, someone opts out, room membership changes, or the bot restarts. Your chart-aware turns include only your question and gate context, never shared conversation history. The commands and replies are visible to everyone in this room and may remain in room history. Use only your own gate list and only if you want the room and provider to receive it.

Opting out clears the bot’s short in-memory context. A request already sent to the AI provider cannot be recalled. Avoid sensitive health, reproductive, child, or intimate details in this prototype.
